## Image credits

Cover thumbnails and character illustrations preserve their original proportions. The character gallery shows each artist’s name below the image; full compositions, signatures, and original lettering are retained. Artist-owned posts and official publications are linked above. For the two game illustrations, the catalog also identifies the game-asset archive used to retrieve the original artwork.

Artwork belongs to the respective artists and rights holders. The credited images are not covered by this repository’s code license. Original-post references, artist profiles, image sources, and provenance notes are recorded in `.github/profile-media.json`.
